2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

None. RACE ON does not require registration, login, or a profile. You do not need to provide your name, email, phone, or address to play.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you play, your device and the third-party services listed in Section 4 may automatically collect:

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language, screen size, and app version.
  • Device identifiers: advertising ID (for example Google Advertising ID) and app-instance ID, used only for ads, analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • Usage data: which screens / tracks you open, race duration, crashes, and performance (frame rate, errors).
  • Approximate location: only if an ad or analytics SDK infers coarse region from IP address. We do not collect precise GPS location.

2.3 Information we do NOT collect

  • No name, email, phone, address, or contacts.
  • No precise location (GPS).
  • No photos, videos, microphone recordings, or files on your device.
  • No payment card details - payments are handled by Google Play if you make an in-app purchase.
  • No health, biometric, or sensitive personal data.

5. Data sharing

We share data only as described in Section 4, and only with the service providers needed to run the game, show ads, and fix crashes. We do not share data with data brokers. We do not share precise location or contacts with anyone.

Third-party SDKs may collect and process data under their own policies. You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalisation (steps vary by Android version).

6. Data retention

  • On-device game data (progress, settings) stays on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app.
  • Analytics / crash data is kept by the analytics provider for the period stated in their policy, typically up to 14 months, and is aggregated / anonymised.
  • Advertising data is kept by the ad provider under its own retention policy.
